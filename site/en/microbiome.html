What exact role does the microbiome play in our health?

Serious leads

Why it matters

Gut microbes have been linked to many chronic diseases, from digestive to psychiatric ones, but cause and effect is often unclear. Knowing their exact role could make the microbiome a target for new, non-invasive treatments.

What we know

Gut bacteria are linked to obesity, immunity and perhaps mood.

What blocks us

Most links are correlations; proving cause is hard.

Current leads

Controlled trials of fecal transplants, targeted therapeutic bacteria.
