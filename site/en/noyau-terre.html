What exactly is at the center of the Earth?

Serious leads

What we know

A solid iron-nickel inner core sits inside a liquid outer core, and its rotation appears to vary over time.

What blocks us

Only seismic waves can probe it, indirectly.

Current leads

Denser seismometer networks, lab experiments at extreme pressures.

Sub-questions

  • What makes the inner core speed up and then slow down relative to the rest of the planet?
  • Why did the inner core move back along its path more slowly than it moved forward?
  • How do the solid inner core, the liquid outer core and the mantle pull on one another?
