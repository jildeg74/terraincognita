What is dark matter?

Serious leads

What we know

About 85% of the matter in the universe is invisible. We detect it only through its gravitational pull on galaxies and light.

What blocks us

Decades of direct-detection experiments have confirmed nothing; in 2026 LZ reported one candidate event, far below the threshold for a discovery.

Current leads

Massive particles (WIMPs), axions, next-generation underground detectors, or a modification of gravity.

Sub-questions

  • If dark matter is made of WIMPs, could they be lighter or give weaker signals than the simplest models predict?
  • Do puzzles in small galaxies (such as cusp-core and missing satellites) point to dark matter that interacts with itself, or to errors in simulations?
  • Could gravitational waves from black holes reveal dark matter gathered around them?
