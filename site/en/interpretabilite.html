Artificial intelligence

Can we understand what happens inside a neural network?

Serious leads

Why it matters

Understanding what happens inside neural networks could give greater assurance about how AI systems will behave, and could shed light on scientific questions about the nature of intelligence.

What we know

We can isolate internal concepts and circuits in large language models.

What blocks us

Models are huge, and each neuron mixes several concepts.

Current leads

Feature dictionaries, circuit tracing, automated analysis tools.

In depth

Where things stand

Mechanistic interpretability aims to understand the computational mechanisms behind a neural network's capabilities 1. A core difficulty is that single neurons are often polysemantic: they respond to several unrelated features 1. The superposition hypothesis explains this: a network can represent more features than it has dimensions, as long as each feature is active only rarely 1.

Competing ideas

The most popular way to pull these features apart is sparse dictionary learning, often with sparse autoencoders, which break a model's internal activity into many more interpretable parts 1. These methods have limits: inserting such a dictionary into GPT-4 lowered its performance to that of a model trained with about 10% of the compute, and they describe what flows in and out of a model's mechanisms rather than the mechanisms themselves 1.

What would settle it

Success would mean understanding a network well enough to predict or steer its behaviour, and a 2025 review by 29 authors concludes that the field still has considerable distance to cover 1. It groups the open problems into three areas: methods and foundations, applications, and socio-technical questions 1.

Latest developments

In May 2025, Anthropic released open-source tools that build attribution graphs, which partially reveal the steps a model took internally to reach a particular answer 2. They were used to study multi-step reasoning and multilingual representations in the open models Gemma-2-2b and Llama-3.2-1b 2. The company states that understanding of how AI works inside lags far behind the progress in its capabilities 2.
