Why do some people become addicted and others not?

Serious leads

What we know

Addiction rewires the brain's reward circuits, and genes explain part of the risk.

What blocks us

Relapse is common, and the interplay of genes, environment and trauma is poorly understood.

Current leads

GLP-1 drugs that seem to reduce cravings, targeted brain stimulation.

Sub-questions

  • Why do genetic risk scores still predict who will become addicted so poorly?
  • Do chemical marks on DNA (epigenetics) explain lasting addiction in humans, and could they become drug targets?
  • Can deep brain stimulation safely help people with the most severe addictions?
