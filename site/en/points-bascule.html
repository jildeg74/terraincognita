Where are the climate's tipping points?

Serious leads

What we know

Fragile systems have been identified: Atlantic currents, ice sheets, the Amazon. In 2025, scientists concluded that warm-water coral reefs have already crossed theirs.

What blocks us

The exact thresholds are uncertain, and we may find them too late.
