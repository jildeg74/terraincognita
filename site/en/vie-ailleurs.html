Are we alone in the universe?

Serious leads

Why it matters

It would answer a fundamental question: are we alone in the universe? Researchers have described the search for life beyond Earth as one of the most monumental and consequential endeavours humanity has ever embarked on.

What we know

More than 6,000 exoplanets are confirmed, many in the zone where water can be liquid.

What blocks us

No sign of life, or biosignature, has been confirmed beyond Earth.

Current leads

The James Webb telescope, the future Habitable Worlds Observatory, and the Europa Clipper mission to Jupiter's moon Europa.
