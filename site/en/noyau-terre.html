What exactly is at the center of the Earth?

Serious leads

Why it matters

The churning liquid iron around Earth's inner core generates our magnetic field, and changes in the inner core's spin may alter the length of a day by fractions of a second. Knowing what lies at the center would help understand these deep processes.

What we know

A solid iron-nickel inner core sits inside a liquid outer core, and its rotation appears to vary over time.

What blocks us

Only seismic waves can probe it, indirectly.

Current leads

Denser seismometer networks, lab experiments at extreme pressures.
