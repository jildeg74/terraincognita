What lives in the deep ocean?

Serious leads

Why it matters

Human activity already threatens the deep ocean, yet only a tiny fraction of its floor has ever been seen. Knowing what lives there would help people manage and protect it, instead of making decisions without adequate science.

What we know

Most of the deep seafloor has never been explored, and expeditions keep finding new species.

What blocks us

Reaching the depths is slow and expensive.

Current leads

Autonomous underwater robots, environmental DNA collected from seawater.

Sub-questions

  • Which deep-sea places should be explored next to fill the biggest gaps in what we have seen?
  • Can cheaper, autonomous robots make deep-sea exploration fast enough to see much more of the seafloor?
  • How much does our picture of deep-sea life reflect only the few places, like canyons near rich countries, that have been studied most?
