Artificial intelligence

How will AI change work and the economy?

Distant horizon

What we know

Early studies show large productivity gains on some tasks.

What blocks us

Most studies cover a few tasks or companies; the effects on the whole economy are not yet known.

Current leads

Measuring how AI is actually used at work, policies for transitions.

Sub-questions

  • Why do newer, less-skilled workers gain the most from AI assistants, while experts gain little?
  • Do workers who use AI assistants actually learn skills they keep?
  • Why does AI change productivity so differently from one worker to another?

Related questions

  • Why do some nations become rich and others not?
  • How do we build general AI that is reliable and aligned with our interests?
  • Why has productivity growth slowed in rich countries?
