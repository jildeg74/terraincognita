Why do humans cooperate on such a large scale?

Serious leads

What we know

Humans cooperate with strangers far beyond what other species do.

What blocks us

We understand poorly why cooperation sometimes collapses.

Current leads

Evolutionary game theory, cross-cultural experiments.
