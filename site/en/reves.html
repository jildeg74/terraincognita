What are dreams for?

Distant horizon

Why it matters

Frequent nightmares are common in post-traumatic stress disorder and are associated with a higher risk of suicide. Understanding what dreams do could help doctors recognize nightmares as warning signs and improve care for trauma-related symptoms.

What we know

Vivid dreams occur mostly during REM sleep.

What blocks us

There is no agreed function for dreaming.

Current leads

Memory replay, rehearsal of threats, lucid dreamers who can signal from inside a dream.
