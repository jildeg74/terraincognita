Can we decipher lost writing systems such as the Indus script?

Serious leads

What we know

The Indus script, Linear A and Rongorongo are still undeciphered. AI helped read scrolls carbonized by Vesuvius.

What blocks us

Surviving texts are few and short, and the languages behind them are unknown.

Current leads

Statistical and AI pattern analysis, new archaeological finds.

Sub-questions

  • Are the Indus signs and Rongorongo real writing that records a language, or a different kind of symbol system?
  • How can Rongorongo be split into units of language when many of its signs look almost identical?
  • Can computer methods crack a truly unknown script by themselves, or can they only assist human experts?
